🐐 Goat Rearing at Hulagabal

Our goats are raised with the highest standards of natural care in Bijapur.

  • Breeds: Healthy local and Osmanabadi breeds.
  • Diet: 100% natural fodder and grains grown on our own 5 acres.
  • Care: Stress-free environment with regular veterinary check-ups.
  • Availability: We provide goats for breeding and organic manure for farms.

🌾 Crops We Grow at Hulagabal

We cultivate nutritious and quality crops using natural farming methods in Bijapur.

  • 🌱 Chana Dal: Protein-rich pulses grown with healthy soil practices.
  • 🌽 Maize: Fresh maize cultivated with balanced water management.
  • 🌾 Wheat: Premium wheat grown naturally for healthy grains and flour.
  • 🌿 Jowar: Traditional grain grown for food and fodder purposes.
  • 🧅 Onion: Quality onions cultivated with care for freshness and storage.
  • 🌍 Method: Sustainable farming with minimal chemicals and soil care.
